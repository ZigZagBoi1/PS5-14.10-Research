## Relation to #299

`results/p818_fw1400_rva_20261118.md` states: "The CSSFontFaceSet vtable slot
functions are at the same VP-relative offsets on both firmwares. ZigZag's 14.10
gadget chain offsets measured from VP can transfer directly to 14.00." That
statement is correct as written and is not challenged here. It is scoped to
VP-relative vtable slots.

This result bounds the other direction:

| Offset class | 14.10 -> 14.00 | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| VP-relative vtable slots (s0-s7) | transfers | #299, 8/8 slots [M] |
| base-relative import GOT (`+0x3350850`) | does not transfer; hard fault | this result [M] |
| base address (`VP-0xC6A1B28` vs `VP-0x7F00B28` [I]) | does not transfer | #299, different image geometry |

An image-size difference alone does not prove a RVA will fault; the on-device
TRY/RD pair is the evidence here. The size figures only explain why the two
builds cannot share base-relative RVAs.

## Device sequence

Beacons are decoded from URL encoding; order and UID are preserved.

```
D41-DG-WKBASE-0x807055000-VP-0x80ef55b28
D41-DG-START-i0/3
D41-DG-TRY-i0-0x80ef55a28
D41-DG-RD-i0-len128
D41-DG-TRY-i1-0x80a3a5850
```

`0x80ef55b28 - 0x100 = 0x80ef55a28` (control, readable).
`0x807055000 + 0x3350850 = 0x80a3a5850` (14.10 memset slot on 14.00, fault).

No `DG-RD-i1` follows `DG-TRY-i1`. Under the probe's beacon contract this means
the process died at the requested address rather than returning data.

## Read-bracketing rule

Every arbitrary read in this probe is bracketed:

1. `DG-TRY-i<n>-<ta>` immediately before the read.
2. `DG-RD-i<n>-len<N>` immediately after a successful read.

TRY present + RD absent = SIGBUS at `ta`; both present = readable. This replaces
the earlier `setR`+`chk` gate, which was a deterministic self-inflicted fault:
`chk` walks engine-corrupted post-`readStyle` buffers and is a codepoint
oracle, not a readability test.

## Open items (not claimed here)

- 14.00's own import GOT was not located in this run. The earlier scan above
  VP was aimed at the wrong side of the image; a downward scan from the
  executable boundary is still needed.
- Bench VP page-offset modes `0xBE0` (= same vtable `+0xB8`) and `0x8A0`
  (unanchored) are separate from #299's `0xB28` probe and are not graded here.
- Replication across more landings is worthwhile; the fault itself is a single
  device observation.
