NO exploitable corruption detected. The crash is binary:

  - Above ~20,340: GC crashes instantly
  - No "corrupt but survive" window observed

The crash is most likely:
  1. An assertion/RELEASE_ASSERT in JSC's GC constraint solver
  2. Or an out-of-bounds access in the resizable AB tracking structures

This is a DENIAL OF SERVICE bug, not an exploitable memory corruption.

The resizable ArrayBuffer GC bug (p608 series) is:
