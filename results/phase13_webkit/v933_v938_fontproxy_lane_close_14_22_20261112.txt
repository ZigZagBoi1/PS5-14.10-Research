=== LAW (14.22, this port) ===

FontFaceSet consultation happens EXCLUSIVELY at font-property-set time
(eager cascade realization). Sources that match complete synchronously
there; sources that do not match are never consulted (status stays
'unloaded' forever until an explicit fonts.load()). NO settlement
(promise or event) fires during measureText/drawText/metrics in any
probed shape: not the loaded-fulfillment, not loadingdone, not
script/glyph fallback. The only settle point (font-set) is benign -
no stale FontProxy or State reference survives the realloc (S2, R).

The documented reentry of 673f4df5 (then-getter inside
metricsOfPrimaryFont) is STRUCTURALLY UNREACHABLE on 14.22: the UAF
window requires a load to complete while measureText holds the raw
FontProxy*, and on this port loads never complete inside that window.

Consequence: the last public Canvas2D UAF candidate for 14.22 is closed
by device measurement. The 14.22 scoreboard is unchanged: no address
source, no userland exec path; kstuff/etaHEN port remains blocked
without a non-WebKit vulnerability class.
