=== GRADE OF THE ZERO — three statements, one per law ===

L6 (return channel): v911 conforms. The SURV beacon fires on ANY returned forge
read, value recorded bit-exact (Float64Array round-trip, no arithmetic, DAZ-safe
per the forge-read law). Under the board law a returned read of any value,
0.0 included, is an attributed mapped bound at T. Zero SURV over the grid is
therefore zero return-channel events — the sweep did not miss hits through the
value channel.

L7 (getter touch set): conforms by construction. The forge sets m_buffer LE
pair @+112, cap u32 @+120, size u32 @+124; the read consumes exactly
{+112, +124, T+0x30}. Arm B (size=64/cap=64, read at T+63*448+0x30 ~ T+27.6KB)
never fires in practice: it is gated on an arm-A survive, and there were none.
Per the pre-registration this is by design (crash/crash adds no information
once the AB(768)-over-ctx-chunk landing is accepted from v904) — but see the
confound below, which removes that acceptance.

L8 (era posture): the 2026-11-06 CHANGELOG FW-delta pin ("zero-at-free on 14.22
vs benign freed chunks on 14.10, null-spray corpus 21/21") is RETIRED on the
14.10 side per the board's own self-correction: the corpus is survivor-grade
only, and null@+112-after-free on 14.10 is two-bench device-grade (V627 null
+0x30 row; 4TheDoctor p641 v2 4/4 first-read death, no spray, 14.10).
Correspondingly, OUR v909 row is regraded: it is OBSERVER-grade (6/6 crashes at
the first read, before any reclaim), not byte-grade — the "ZEROES the freed
768B ctx chunk" wording in v910_dense_sweep_20261106.txt is an inference from
crash behavior, not a byte readout. The board's open cell (byte-grade
unreclaimed readout, either bench, either class) remains OPEN from our side.

=== SELF-REPORTED CONFOUND — why this zero is not a region verdict ===

v905_band0_exhausted_20261106.txt already retired the +112 forge "until a
positive control exists" (two candidate failures: the AB does not overlay the
freed ctx chunk in a way the read path consumes, or the read path validates
fields the pattern poisons). v911 re-ran that same uncontrolled forge at grid
scale. The grid itself now supplies a third, independent argument that the
confound dominates:

  Band 3 (stack) contains guaranteed-mapped addresses. The band ends exactly at
  0x7fffffffe000 — the classic FreeBSD x86-64 stack top — sampled at 256KB
  over the top 256MB. The main-thread stack occupies multiple MB adjacent to
  the top; several of the 1024 targets must land in stack pages. If the forge
  had ever landed and been consumed, a stack-band read returns (any value) and
  beacons SURV. Zero SURV across 1024 stack targets is inconsistent with a
  working forge and consistent with every one of the ~6300 reads dying at
  null+0x30 (zeroed, unreclaimed or unconsumed ctx chunk) — target-independent,
  exactly the pattern v651_v904 flagged for v902/v903.

Consequence: the ~6300-target grid is an attribution-blind negative (same grade
the CHANGELOG of 2026-11-06 assigned to the ~1400-target sweep). It does not
establish that the swept regions are unmapped, and it does not close
attribution. What it does establish: the two-arm differential, pre-registered
as the decider, cannot decide anything until the forge mechanism itself has a
positive control on 14.22. That control is now the single blocking item —
probe v914 (probes/phase13_webkit/v914_positive_control.html) is designed for
exactly it and needs no absolute address.
